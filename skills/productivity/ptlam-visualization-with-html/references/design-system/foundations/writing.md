# Writing

Prefer direct verbs, concrete nouns, sentence case, and one action per control.
Keep essential labels visible; do not rely on a tooltip for primary meaning. Let
text resize and reflow without truncating instructions or state.

Follow Material's guidance for
[writing best practices](https://m3.material.io/foundations/writing/best-practices),
[text resizing](https://m3.material.io/foundations/writing/text-resizing), and
[text truncation](https://m3.material.io/foundations/writing/text-truncation).
